- **Fixed** A fainted Pokémon stays fainted. HP Up on a fainted Pokémon brought it back with a few HP, and a Pomeg Berry gave full HP to a fainted one or one at 1 HP. ## 2026-10-18
- **Fixed** A Pokémon a script gives an item to no longer loses it when the battle starts (reported: "Light ball is not attached to pikachu when fighting him in the abandoned ship. An Oran berry is in its place."). The Abandoned Ship's Pikachu (Lv 34) is set up holding the Light Ball, but the game's wild held-item roll (SetWildMonHeldItem, 0x0806EA68) runs as every wild battle starts - twice in this hack, once from its own battle setup and once from the game's - and never looks whether the Pokémon already holds something: each time, 50 in 100 it gave Pikachu's common item (Oran Berry), 5 its rare one (Light Ball), 45 left it. Measured on 40 battles with the same 40 random seeds: Light Ball 13 times and Oran Berry 27 before, Light Ball 40 times after. A bug of the original hack: its own ROM does the same (Oran Berry 29 times of 40; vanilla Emerald never gives a scripted Pokémon an item, so the roll never mattered there). The same fix holds for the other 47 such battles - the Totems in the Secret Bases now always hold their Z-Crystal in battle (in the hack's own ROM too, Gumshoos held its Normalium Z 9 times of 40, a Pecha Berry 27, nothing 4; the Z-Crystal you are given for beating a Totem was never affected: the script gives it from var 0x8000, 623 + the type, whatever the Totem holds), Giratina's Griseous Orb, Area Zero's Leftovers, Kitakami's Black Sludge and the rest; a species with no wild items used to lose the item to nothing - and for DexNav's held item, which the roll replaced the same way. A Pokémon that holds nothing is rolled for as before (same odds: 40 battles gave the same counts within one). `patches/wilditem/`
