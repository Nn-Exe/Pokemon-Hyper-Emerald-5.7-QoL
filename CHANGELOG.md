# Changelog

One line per change, newest first. The how and why of each change is in [docs/NOTES.md](docs/NOTES.md); each
patch folder has its own notes. Tags: **Added** new feature · **Fixed** bug fix · **Changed** · **Tool** for modders.

## 2026-10-02
- **Fixed** Guide: the family lists only show what a player can get. Two forms were on them that cannot be had: Eternamax Eternatus (the game does not let Eternatus Dynamax, and nothing else leads to the form) and Kyurem-WB (nothing makes or gives it). Both are only ever on a trainer's team; they are off every "Evolution & Forms" section now, and their own pages say so and name the trainers. Gigantamax Charizard no longer names the Wishing Piece: the item exists, but nothing in the game hands it out. Ash's Pikachu, listed without a way before, has one: beat Ash at the Battle Frontier and he puts a hat on your Pikachu.
- **Tool** `tools/romdata/obtainable.py` audits it: for every Pokémon and form, is there a way to it, and does what the way needs (an item, a move, the other half of a fusion) have a source. 1,168 of 1,170 have one; all 110 items that forms need and all 38 that evolutions need have a source. It exits 1 when something on the lists has no way, so it can be run after a new ROM.
- **Added** Guide: every form says how you get it. The Pokédex's "Evolution & Forms" section and each form's own page name what brings the form about, read from the game's tables and battle code (`tools/romdata/forms.py`): Mega Stones and Primal orbs, Max Soup for the 34 Gigantamax forms (the cook on Champion Island, 3 Max Mushrooms), held items (Arceus's Plates, Silvally's Memories, Genesect's Drives, the Griseous Orb, Adamant Crystal and Lustrous Globe, Mewtwo's two armors, the Dusk Stone for Shadow Lugia, the Rusty Sword and Shield), signature moves (Solgaleo, Lunala, Marshadow, Xerneas, Keldeo, Meloetta), abilities (Zen Mode, Stance Change, Schooling, Shields Down, Disguise, Ice Face, Gulp Missile, Hunger Switch, Battle Bond, Power Construct, Flower Gift), Bag items (Gracidea, Prison Bottle, Reveal Glass, the Nectars, Rotom Catalog, Deoxys Meteor, Zygarde Cube), fusions (DNA Splicers, N-Solarizer, N-Lunarizer, Unity Reins) and two side-quest rewards. 179 form changes; 108 forms are marked battle-only.
- **Fixed** Guide: forms that sat under the wrong name. Ash-Greninja and the Battle Bond Greninja it comes from were swapped, as were Eiscue and its Noice Face, and Cramorant and its Gulping form (the wild-encounter tables said "Eiscue (Noice Face)" and "Cramorant (Gulping)" for the ordinary ones); Deoxys's forms are Attack, Normal and Defense (Deoxys itself is the Speed Forme, as in Emerald); the yellow Toxtricity is the Amped one. Settled by base stats, front pictures and the battle code that swaps them. Named for the first time: Mega Flygon and Typhlosion (Burning Soul). Hisuian Sneasel now sits with Sneasel (the ROM's number table gives it Sawsbuck's), and the hack's four restored fossil Pokémon no longer appear as forms of Pikachu (the table gives all four its number).
- **Tool** `tools/romdata/forms.py`: every form change in the ROM as one list, with the table or routine behind each kind in its docstring, and assertions so a ROM that moved them stops the build.
- **Added** Guide: a Pokédex. An index of all 905 species and their 263 Mega, Gigantamax, regional and alternate forms (search by name or number, filter by type and form, sort by any stat), and a page for each one: front sprite with a shiny toggle, types, base stats, abilities with the hidden one marked, training and breeding facts, the Pokédex entry, the evolution family with every method, where to find it (its own encounters, or the earlier stage to start from and the steps to evolve it), and its level-up, TM/HM and egg moves. With it come a Moves page (723 moves) and an Abilities page (268 abilities and who has each). Every Pokémon name on the site now opens its Pokédex page, and search covers Pokémon, moves and abilities.
- **Fixed** Guide: "960 Pokédex entries" was the size of the game's entry table, not the number of Pokémon. There are 905 species (Bulbasaur to Enamorus); slots 906-960 are unused. The Features page, the Pokémon Finder and the README now say 905.
- **Fixed** Notes: the hack does have hidden abilities. The ability bytes in the base-stats table are left over; the game reads abilities from a table of three u16 per species at 0x097A0000 (two regular, one hidden), through a hook on GetAbilityBySpecies (0x0806B694 -> 0x09D73A80). 876 Pokémon and forms have a hidden ability. `docs/NOTES.md` and `docs/DEXNAV-PROGRESS.md` said there were none.
- **Tool** Guide: Gigantamax forms are linked to their species from the game's own battle-form table (0x08C9A3B0: species, the slot it turns into, held item; 34 Gigantamax rows plus Zacian and Zamazenta with the Rusty Sword and Shield) instead of by matching names. The evolution table only knows Charizard's. The two agree on all 34; Zacian and Zamazenta now show their Crowned forms in the evolution section.
- **Tool** `tools/build_pokedex.py` mines species, abilities, moves, evolutions, learnsets, TM compatibility, egg moves and Pokédex entries into `site/src/data/{pokedex,moves,abilities}.json`; its docstring lists where each table is. `tools/build_sprites.py` also writes a front picture and a shiny one for every species (`site/public/sprites/front/`).
- **Changed** The guide website was rebuilt as an Astro project (`site/`, built into `docs/`): a home page, a guides hub with ten guides (the walkthrough in three parts, install, controls, mechanics, legendary locations, Mega Stones and key items, side content, Journal objectives), a wiki (wild encounters, a Pokémon finder, boss teams, every trainer, scripted encounters), features, FAQ, changelog, gallery and about pages, site-wide search, dark mode and a phone layout. The old page addresses (`trainers.html`, `getting-started.html`…) redirect to the new ones. The Features page, which still described v1.0, now covers v1.7.
- **Added** Guide: the game's own sprites - Pokémon menu icons, trainer pictures and item icons, cut from the ROM's tables into three sheets (`tools/build_sprites.py`) - on every team, encounter table and item list.
- **Added** Guide: alternate forms have their own names. The ROM names every form after its base species, so the site printed "Marowak (alt. form)"; it now prints Alolan Marowak, Mega Charizard X, Gigantamax Snorlax, Hisuian Zoroark… (`tools/romdata/species_display.py`, identified from the icons), and names the table cuts to 10-13 characters are spelt out (`display_names.py`).
- **Added** Guide: 54 short gameplay clips cut from the six README montage GIFs (`tools/build_clips.py`, 27 MB of GIF -> 9 MB of MP4 loaded on demand).
- **Fixed** Guide: the God-King Cyrus team on the Lost Artifacts page showed "team not found" (the trainer is named "God Cyrus" in the ROM, so it was missing from the boss list).
- **Tool** `tools/build_site_content.py` exports the Journal's objectives and the side-content list for the site; it replaces `tools/build_journal_page.py`. `tools/build_search_index.py` is gone (the site builds its own index). `tools/build_audit_page.py` now writes its local page to `tools/romdata/out/`.
