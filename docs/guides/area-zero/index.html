Area Zero

The Paldea area behind the black hole in Mossdeep City. The way in, the story with Arven, Nemona and Penny, the Titans, the Zero Lab, the Paradox Pokémon and the legends you can catch once it is over.

Area Zero is an optional post-game area added in v1.9: Paldea’s Great Crater, reached through a black hole in Mossdeep City once Team Rainbow Rocket is beaten. It has a short story of its own, the twenty Paradox Pokémon, and the Gen 9 legends to catch when the story is over. Everything here is level 63 to 80.

Where this page comes from

Read from the v1.9 game: the area’s scripts (who says what, what each step needs, the levels they set), its wild encounter tables and the trainers’ teams. The Journal follows the story too: seven rows tagged optional in its Post-game chapter, listed on the Journal page.

Getting there

  • Jacq, a teacher from Paldea’s academy, stands in Mossdeep City from the start of the game, on the grass by the sea below the Space Center (down the little stairs). He has brought three students to Hoenn on a field study: Arven, Nemona and Penny.
  • Once Team Rainbow Rocket is beaten, a black hole opens beside him and his students are gone. Talk to him, then step into the hole.
  • You land on the crater wall. Research Station No. 1 is on its middle ledge (Penny hides there), so is an icy cave, and the way down into the crater is on the bottom ledge.

The crater, in two times

The crater exists twice: in the past and in the future, with their own colours, music and wild Pokémon. A time rift hangs in the air in each (cold blue in the past, warm amber in the future): press A, answer “Step through?”, and you stand on the same spot in the other time. The places are the same in both:

  • Research Station No. 2: the house on the shelf below the stairs into the crater.
  • Research Station No. 3: the house on the knoll in the east of the crater.
  • Research Station No. 4: above the waterfall. Surf across the pond and climb it (Waterfall).
  • Arven’s picnic: by the big lake in the south.
  • The Titans’ den: on the crater’s west side.
  • The Crystal Cavern: the cave in the crater’s north-west wall.
  • The Zero Lab: the shutter in the crater’s north-east wall.

The story

The Journal lists these steps in this order; the middle three can be done in any order.

1. Penny

Find Penny in Research Station No. 1 on the wall’s middle ledge. She says where the other two went (Arven down into the crater, Nemona off to battle anything that moves) and that the professors left logs in the stations.

2. Arven’s sandwiches

Arven is at his picnic by the big lake, nursing a worn-out Koraidon in the past and a Miraidon in the future. His sandwiches need Sour Herba Mystica from the Titans:

  • In the past, beat the Titan Great Tusk twice in the den on the crater’s west side (Lv 68, then Lv 72 holding Leftovers), and bring Arven its herb: Koraidon gets up.
  • Step through a time rift to the future and do the same with Iron Treads (Lv 68, then Lv 72): Miraidon gets up.

A Titan battle takes no Poké Balls, and losing it does not black you out: you can try again.

3. Nemona

Nemona is in the future, in the tall grass south of the waterfall’s pond, battling every Iron Paradox she finds. Beat her.

4. The four logs

Each research station holds a recorded log from the professors: play the recorder on the left counter of all four (No. 4 is the one above the waterfall). Then talk to Penny in Station No. 1: she unlocks the Zero Lab’s door.

5. The Zero Lab

Once the door is unlocked, both legends are on their feet and Nemona is beaten, Arven and Nemona go and open the shutter in the crater’s north-east wall by themselves. Go in and talk to the machine. The professors’ AIs battle you one after the other, then the Paradise Protection Protocol sends out the lab’s own Koraidon and Miraidon in a Double Battle.

6. Home

The machine stops and everyone goes home through the hole. In Mossdeep City Jacq thanks you with a Master Ball; then he and his students go back in and live in the research stations.

After the story

  • Koraidon and Miraidon wait at the picnic spot, each in its own time, and the rest of the Gen 9 legends appear: three in each crater, two in the Crystal Cavern, one in the icy cave, three in the Sealed Vault behind the Crystal Cavern’s second cave mouth (it opens now) and Terapagos in the Underdepths, down the cavern’s ladder. Penny, back in Station No. 1, says where.
  • Each is met once. Caught or knocked out, it is gone for good, so save first.
  • The Sealed Vault has a floor of polished crystal: you slide over it until something stops you, as on ice. Wo-Chien, Ting-Lu and Chi-Yu wait behind crystals along the back wall; out on the floor stand three stakes, marked with a bundle of tablets, a vessel on three legs and a string of curved beads. Come to a stop beside a stake and pull it, and its legend’s crystal bursts. A pulled stake no longer stops you.
  • Nemona lives in Research Station No. 3 and battles you again, with the same team.

Koraidon and Miraidon

Lv 75ROM

Area Zero, at Arven’s picnic spot by the big lake once the story is over: Koraidon in the crater in the past, Miraidon in the crater in the future. During the story they are worn out, and Arven’s sandwiches get them up again. The Zero Lab’s own pair (Lv 78) is the team of the Paradise Protection Protocol in the finale.

The wild Paradox Pokémon

Lv 63–67ROM

Area Zero’s tall grass at any time: the six ancient ones in the crater in the past, the six iron ones in the crater in the future. Iron Bundle also turns up in the icy cave. Great Tusk and Iron Treads are the story’s Titans too (Lv 68 and 72), which cannot be caught.

Okidogi, Munkidori, Fezandipiti, Ogerpon and Pecharunt

–ROM

Not in this version. The game has them as Pokémon, but they live in Kitakami, which it does not have, and nothing in the game meets them. The Quest Log lists them as Locked and leaves them out of its count.

Items

  • Booster Energy: an item ball in the crater in the past, one in the crater in the future and one in the Crystal Cavern. The Zero Lab’s supply terminal sells more, ¥5,000 each.
  • Never-Melt Ice: an item ball in the icy cave.
  • Master Ball: Jacq’s thanks at the end of the story.

The rest of Gen 9

v1.9 has every Gen 9 Pokémon, 127 slots in all, at their official Pokédex numbers (Sprigatito is No. 906, Pecharunt No. 1025). Outside Area Zero:

  • The other Gen 9 Pokémon, Paldean Wooper and the three Paldean Tauros live in the wild all over Hoenn, without taking any of the game’s own Pokémon away. Each one’s Pokédex page says where; the Pokémon finder searches them all.
  • The Lilycove Department Store, 4F, sells the six new evolution items alongside the old ones: Auspicious Armor and Malicious Armor (Charcadet; ¥3,000 each), Syrupy Apple (Applin; ¥2,200), Metal Alloy (Duraludon; ¥3,000), Unremarkable Teacup (Poltchageist; ¥1,600) and Leader's Crest (Bisharp; ¥3,000).
  • Older families gained their Gen 9 evolutions: Primeape → Annihilape, Girafarig → Farigiraf, Dunsparce → Dudunsparce, Bisharp → Kingambit, Applin → Dipplin → Hydrapple, Duraludon → Archaludon, Paldean Wooper → Clodsire. How each evolves here is on its Pokédex page.
  • The legends’ seven signature moves (Collision Course, Electro Drift, Ruination, Ivy Cudgel, Hydro Steam, Psyblade, Glaive Rush) and their abilities, Protosynthesis and Quark Drive among them, have code of their own. The other new moves are copies of moves the game already has that work the same way, and the other new abilities stand in for ones it has (Earth Eater works as Levitate). Some do less than in Scarlet and Violet: Rage Fist and Last Respects are plain hits. Each move’s and ability’s description on the Moves and Abilities pages says what it does in this game.
  • Okidogi, Munkidori, Fezandipiti, Ogerpon and Pecharunt are in the game’s data but cannot be met: they live in Kitakami, which this game does not have. The Quest Log shows them as Locked.
A save that has been opened in v1.9 stays on v1.9

v1.9 moves a save’s Gen 9 Pokédex marks to where the longer Pokédex keeps them, the first time the save is loaded. Do not take that save back to v1.8 or older. Keep a copy of your .sav before you update.
