Move Tutors

All 41 move tutors, read from their scripts. Where each one stands, the moves they teach, what they charge and the favour some want first, then every tutor move with who teaches it, and the Move Relearner.

Last updated October 24, 2026

The game has a list of 127 tutor moves, and 41 tutors teach every one of them. Some teach for free, as often as you like. Sixteen want ¥5,000 a move once you have done them a favour, four take coloured Shards, and two in the Battle Frontier price their moves in Battle Points. Eight more belong to the story or to one Pokémon.

Where this list comes from

Each tutor was found and followed through the scripts of the v2.0 game: the moves come from the list the script hands to the game, the price from what it takes, and the favour from the checks it makes before the menu opens. Chase’s partner moves are taught another way, by a routine of the hack’s own, and were read from it. Each picture shows the map around the tutor, with their tile framed. Click a picture to enlarge it.

A Pokémon can learn a tutor move only if the move is on that species’ own list. The party screen a tutor opens marks each Pokémon as able to learn it or not, and every Pokédex page now lists the tutor moves that species can learn, with the cheapest way to get each one. To look a move up, go to Who teaches each move.

Battle Frontier tutors

The old Beauty and the old Swimmer sit side by side in the Battle Frontier’s Lounge 7, ten moves each.

The Battle Points are never taken

Each move shows a Battle Point price, and you need that many Battle Points to choose it, but they stay in your pocket: the step that should take them lost the line that does it in the hack’s own script (the unpatched hack has the same bytes). Tested in the v2.0 game: 100 Battle Points before a lesson, 100 after. The same slip sends you back to the wrong menu: after a lesson the menu that comes back is always the Swimmer’s, so talk to the Beauty again for hers.

Chase teaches them without the party screen’s able / not able marks: he checks the species only after you have picked the Pokémon and the move it forgets, and any other Pokémon is “not compatible”.

The Move Relearner

The patch adds Moves to the party menu: pick a Pokémon, choose Moves, and it can relearn a move for free, anywhere. The list holds the level-up moves up to its current level and, since v2.0, its egg moves too. The game’s own Move Relearners, in Fallarbor Town and in Pastoria City, open the same screen for a Heart Scale. Tutor moves are not on that list: a Pokémon that forgets one has to go back to a tutor.
