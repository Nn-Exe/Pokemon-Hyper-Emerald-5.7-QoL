Mega Stones, Z-Crystals & Key Items

Z-Crystals

All eighteen type Z-Crystals are held by Totem Pokémon at level 80, each in a hidden base-style spot in Hoenn. The species and crystals are read from the ROM; the route hints are from community guides. If you beat a Totem without getting its crystal, Selene on Steven’s Island can re-awaken it. Collecting all 18 is required to reach Team Rainbow Rocket’s castle and Necrozma.

The 17 Plates

Needed for Dialga, Palkia and Arceus. Waji’s riddles and the resolved locations are on the Lost Artifacts page. In short:

  • Hoenn: Fist (Sealed Chamber), Sky (the Wingull house on Route 119), Earth (Granite Cave 1F), Iron and Dread (Mauville Rocket base), Flame (Mt. Chimney), Splash (underwater on Route 126), Meadow (Route 120), Zap (New Mauville), Draco (the Meteor Temple).
  • Sinnoh: Toxic (the marsh on Route 212), Stone (Oreburgh Mine), Insect and Mind (Solaceon Ruins), Spooky (Lost Tower), Icicle (Route 217 hut), Pixie (Mt. Coronet).
  • The Blank Plate comes from Volo.

HMs

Cut (Rustboro), Flash (Granite Cave miner), Rock Smash (Mauville), Strength, Surf (Wally’s father after Norman), Fly, Dive, Waterfall (after Rayquaza calms the weather). With the patch, owning the HM and its badge is enough: Cut, Strength, Rock Smash, Surf, Waterfall and Dive work even when nobody in your party can learn them, and Flash is offered in the party menu inside a dark cave. In Sinnoh an HM is stored in the Team Galactic warehouse, and Rock Climb is available to Sinnoh trainers and via the ride gear.
