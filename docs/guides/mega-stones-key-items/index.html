Z-Crystals

Read from the game’s scripts. Each of the eighteen type Z-Crystals belongs to a Totem Pokémon, Lv 80, that waits in a Secret Base room. A room has three or four ways in: the Secret Base spots pictured for it below (a big tree, a shrub or an indent in a rock wall). Use Secret Power on any one of them and go in.

  • The Totems appear once you have reported back to Anabel on Steven’s Island from your first journey through an Ultra Wormhole. Gladion is then back at the big tree on Route 111: the first Totem, Wishiwashi, is inside it, and the other seventeen do not show until it is beaten.
  • The room asks “Want to battle the Totem?”. The game refuses (“Can’t use some moves!”) while a Pokémon in your party knows Destiny Bond, Perish Song, Pain Split or Leech Seed.
  • Win, and the crystal is handed to you; the Totem does not come back. If you won and the crystal is not in your Bag, Selene on Steven’s Island wakes that Totem again.

HMs

Cut (Rustboro), Flash (Granite Cave miner), Rock Smash (Mauville), Strength, Surf (Wally’s father after Norman), Fly, Dive, Waterfall (after Rayquaza calms the weather). With the patch, owning the HM and its badge is enough: Cut, Strength, Rock Smash, Surf, Waterfall and Dive work even when nobody in your party can learn them, and Flash is offered in the party menu inside a dark cave. In Sinnoh an HM is stored in the Team Galactic warehouse, and Rock Climb is available to Sinnoh trainers and via the ride gear.
