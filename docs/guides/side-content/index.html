Side Content & Gift Pokémon

Everything the Journal does not point you to. The Hisuian trades, gift Pokémon, other regions' hidden starters, strong one-off battles, secret trials and side stories.

Last updated October 2, 2026

The Journal follows the story. This page is the rest: every optional battle, gift and scene found by reading the game’s scripts. In game, the Quest Log’s Side Content chapter ticks most of these off as you do them, with a red star on the ones whose Pokémon is always shiny.

Where this list comes from

Each row was read from the scripts of v5.7 bugfix 2, not from a wiki, so it lists what the game can actually do. Levels are the ones the script sets.

Hidden powers

Two secret trials that give your Pokémon a power, and what follows them.

WhereWhat
Route 112 (foot of Mt. Chimney, west side)A hidden cave into a sealed part of the Magma Hideout. Take TM53, then face the flame creature and do NOT choose to leave: the Crimson Apostle (a Flareon serving the Crimson Nova) runs three rounds of hologram battles (trainers 1155-1157) and awakens the Burning Soul. One try only: on the way out the cave collapses (0x42E2) whether you finished or fled; 0x4301 is set only if you finished. Lore: a Typhlosion trainer passed the same trial on Stark Mountain; Flint (Test of Heart) remembers a Johto girl's very fast Typhlosion.
Champion IslandBeat Koga (trainer 1160): he gives his mark and points to a hidden village through the forest of Route 102.
Koga's Village (hidden, via Route 102)Elder Koga's succession trial: only one Greninja, some items banned, two stages (trainers 1159, 1158). Your Greninja gains its hero form, like Ash's.
Route 101Ash from Pallet Town comes for a 2-vs-2 battle with you and your rival; a red-haired passerby (-Echo-) joins and drops a cartridge and a note.
Steven's IslandThe northeast corner of Steven's residence: -Echo- / -Hibiki- (trainer 1016). A book there by Gengyi Xiang adds entries as you earn each power.
