Kitakami

Mossui Town in Kitakami, by bus from Mossdeep City once Area Zero is done. The Teal Mask story with Carmine, Kieran, the Loyal Three and Ogerpon, then Mochi Mayhem and Pecharunt.

Kitakami is an optional post-game area added in v2.0: Mossui Town, a valley of red maples with a festival market, Kitakami Hall and Loyalty Plaza. It holds the Teal Mask story in eight chapters with Carmine, Kieran and Ogerpon, then Mochi Mayhem with your friends from Paldea and Pecharunt. Its battles are level 70 to 82.

Where this page comes from

Read from the v2.0 game: Kitakami’s scripts (who says what, what each step needs, the levels they set), its wild table and the trainers’ teams.

Getting there

  • Finish Area Zero’s story first (the Area Zero guide): once Jacq has gone back in, Briar stands beside the black hole in Mossdeep City, on the grass below the Space Center.
  • Talk to her and say Yes: her bus takes you to Mossui Town’s bus stop. She waits there to take you back to Mossdeep whenever you like.
  • Kitakami Hall is in the north-west of the town, Loyalty Plaza in its grove in the north-east, the market street down the middle, Carmine and Kieran’s house on the west and the Pokémon Center on the east.

The Teal Mask

Every story battle here is fought with no white-out: lose and you are healed, and you can try again on the spot.

  2. Kitakami Hall, the Mask Hall: the Loyal Three’s masks stand in glass cases, and the caretaker before the stage asks you to read the legend’s three signboards (notice boards under a red roof).

  3. The signboards: one among the rice fields in the west, one among those in the east, one at the north end of the avenue, west of Loyalty Plaza. Kieran waits by each in turn; read it with him there.

  4. Loyalty Plaza: Kieran tells you about the ogre before the Loyal Three’s stone statues. Evening falls, and the Festival of Masks begins.

  5. The festival: Carmine waits at the market gate. Ogerpon runs into you in the crowd and drops the Teal Mask. From now on the stalls are shops (below).

  6. Night: at Carmine and Kieran’s house their grandfather tells the true story of the ogre. The mask is mended overnight, then gone.

  7. Kieran waits at Loyalty Plaza with the mask and battles you. The Loyal Three wake, Ogerpon comes, and the three are driven off with her masks.

After the story Kieran’s grandfather, at home, changes the mask of the first Ogerpon in your party (Teal, Wellspring, Hearthflame or Cornerstone), and the Loyal Three can be caught where they hide.

Mochi Mayhem

After the Teal Mask, Briar has a letter from Kieran, in Mossdeep City or at Mossui’s bus stop (the mochi stall’s vendor tells you she was looking for you). Mossui is under a spell of mochi, and it stays night there until it is over:

  • In the market, Kieran meets you with Arven, Nemona and Penny; Carmine will not stop dancing. Nemona runs off for mochi.
  • Wake the apple farmer by the paddies and the mother on the street by talking to them, then Kieran’s grandparents in front of Kitakami Hall, then Arven and Penny in the market. A peach-shaped Pokémon shows itself over the market and flies off.
  • At Loyalty Plaza Nemona battles you. Then Pecharunt battles you (Lv 82, no Poké Balls): beat it and it is yours. With a full party it goes to the PC.
  • The morning after, Kieran and Carmine see you off at the bus stop.

The legends

The Quest Log’s Legends chapter counts all five of Kitakami’s legends: 124 in all.

Ogerpon

Lv 75ROM

Mossui Town, Loyalty Plaza, at the end of the Teal Mask story: after Kieran’s second battle she fights you in each of her four masks (no Poké Balls), then once more to be caught. Afterwards Kieran’s grandfather, at home, changes the mask of the first Ogerpon in your party: Teal, Wellspring, Hearthflame or Cornerstone.

Okidogi, Munkidori and Fezandipiti

Lv 75ROM

Mossui Town, once the Teal Mask story is over, each where it hid during the hunt: Okidogi just east of Loyalty Plaza’s statues, Munkidori by Loyalty Plaza’s sign, Fezandipiti by Kitakami Hall’s sign. During the story you battle them for the masks with no Poké Balls; afterwards each is met once: if it faints, it is gone for good.

Pecharunt

Lv 82ROM

Mossui Town at night, in Mochi Mayhem after the Teal Mask story: Briar has a letter from Kieran, in Mossdeep City or in Mossui. Wake the town from the mochi’s spell; once Nemona is beaten at Loyalty Plaza, Pecharunt battles you (no Poké Balls) and is yours once beaten. With a full party it goes to the PC.
