Why 772 and not the whole Pokédex

The game’s Pokédex has 960 entries. This page lists only what can be met as a wild encounter: 707species, plus 65 regional forms (Alolan, Galarian, Hisuian…) that have their own encounters and are counted separately. The rest are starters, evolutions, gifts and trades, or scripted battles: seeScripted Encounters,Legendary Locations andSide Content & Gift Pokémon.
