How Berries work here

  • Trees. A new game plants 80 of the 120 trees with ripe Berries; the others start as soft soil. Picking takes every Berry on the tree and leaves soft soil. Plant a Berry there and it grows more of the same: in the words of Floaroma’s flower shop, “If you plant a Berry, a few days later you can harvest lots of Berries! That way, your Berries will slowly multiply.” Some Sinnoh trees are one plot with a tree in Hoenn (“one plot with …” above): the game keeps a single tree for both, so picking one leaves the other bare too.
  • Only Emerald’s 43 grow true. The planting code knows Berries No. 1-43 (Cheri to Enigma) and turns any other into a Cheri Berry: plant one of the hack’s 24 (Occa to Maranga) and Cheri Berries come up.
  • Daily gifts come back every day: the game clears their flags when the day changes.
  • Shops. Floaroma Town · Pretty Petal Flower Shop sells every one of the 67. Route 104 · Pretty Petal Flower Shop sells 7: Leppa, Pomeg, Kelpsy, Qualot, Hondew, Grepa, Tamato.
  • Wild Pokémon hold the first item of their species half the time and the second one time in twenty (the percentages above); a species whose two items are the same always holds it.

Read from the game’s scripts: the trees and what the new-game script plants in each (checked against a new game in the emulator), every person, item ball, hidden item and shop list that hands over a Berry, and the wild encounter tables with each species’ held items. People and items the game keeps where nobody can walk are left out. Not listed: four more entries in the Berries pocket (Mago Berry, Wiki Berry, Aguav Berry, Tanga Berry, No. 68-71), copies that nothing in the game hands out; and Dad’s Enigma Berry in the Petalburg Gym, which he gives only to a save holding an e-Reader Berry, something no part of the game itself provides.
