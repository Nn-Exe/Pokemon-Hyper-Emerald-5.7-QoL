Mega Aerodactyl

Fossil PokémonMega

Aerodactyl is a Pokémon from the age of dinosaurs. It was regenerated from DNA extracted from amber. It is imagined to have been the king of the skies.

Where to Find Mega Aerodactyl

Mega Aerodactyl is a battle-only form of Aerodactyl, so it has no encounter of its own. It Mega Evolves in battle while holding the Aerodactylite. See Mega Stones & Key Items.
