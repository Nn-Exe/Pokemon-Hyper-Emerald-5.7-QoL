Primal Kyogre

Kyogre has appeared in mythology as the creator of the sea. After long years of feuding with Groudon, it took to sleep at the bottom of the sea.

Where to Find Primal Kyogre

Primal Kyogre is a battle-only form of Kyogre, so it has no encounter of its own. Primal Reversion: it enters battle holding the Blue Orb. See Mega Stones & Key Items.

Start with Kyogre

Legendary Locations: Kyogre

Marine Cave (Route 125) after Archie. Holds the Blue Orb.
