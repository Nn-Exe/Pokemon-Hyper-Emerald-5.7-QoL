Hidden powers

Two secret trials that give your Pokémon a power, and what follows them.

Route 112 (foot of Mt. Chimney, west side)A hidden cave into a sealed part of the Magma Hideout. Take TM53, then face the flame creature and do NOT choose to leave: the Crimson Apostle (a Flareon serving the Crimson Nova) runs three rounds of hologram battles (trainers 1155-1157) and awakens the Burning Soul. One try only: on the way out the cave collapses (0x42E2) whether you finished or fled; 0x4301 is set only if you finished.
