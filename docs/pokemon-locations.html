Pokémon locations

Every wild encounter table in the ROM: grass, surfing, Rock Smash and fishing, with levels and slot odds. Type a Pokémon or a place.

How to read the odds

Percentages are the fixed slot odds of the Emerald engine (grass: 20, 20, 10, 10, 10, 10, 5, 5, 4, 4, 1, 1). Fishing rows are grouped by rod. "Rate" is the per-step encounter rate for that terrain. Static encounters, gifts, trades and legendaries are on the Legendary Pokémon page and in the walkthrough.
