Unofficial player’s guide

Two regions. Sixteen gyms. One guide for all of it.

Pokémon Hyper Emerald: Lost Artifacts is an Emerald hack with all of Hoenn and Sinnoh, every Pokémon through Generation 8, and a post-game longer than the main story.

This guide covers the English + quality-of-life patch, a walkthrough from Littleroot Town to the last battle in Hisui, and reference tables read straight from the game’s own data.

The English + QoL patch

The whole game in English, with the rough edges filed off.

About 6,000 strings translated, a DexNav, a Journal that names your next objective, a map of Sinnoh you can fly from, a key-item ring with the PC in the middle, and a Bag that holds 200 items.
