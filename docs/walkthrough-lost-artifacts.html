Walkthrough · Part 3

Lost Artifacts questline

The story that names the hack: seventeen Plates scattered across two regions, a rift in space-time, Giratina and Arceus, and a trip to ancient Hisui to face Volo. This is the last and hardest content in the game. The order below follows the game's scripts.

What you need before you start
  • The Sinnoh League cleared: that opens the way up Mt. Coronet to the Spear Pillar.
  • A strong level-100 team. Volo's final team is six level-100 legendaries: Hoopa, Eternatus, Dialga, Palkia, Giratina and Regigigas.
  • Keep every Plate in your Bag once you have it. Arceus and Cogita both check for all seventeen.

1. Waji at the Spear Pillar

Climb Mt. Coronet to the Spear Pillar. Waji, the green-haired man from Petalburg Woods who gave you the Sinnoh ticket, is waiting: "Didn't I tell you someone would give you guidance at the Spear Pillar?" Say yes when he asks whether to start searching and he describes the hiding place of every Plate you are still missing, in riddles ("It might take a while, so take your own notes"). Talk to him again for an updated list. When you have them all: "bring every tablet and step onto this altar, maybe something will happen."

Battled Giratina already?

If you battled Giratina in the Distortion World during the Hoenn post-game, Waji skips the hints and sends you straight to Arceus. You still need all seventeen Plates, so use the table below.

3. The altar and the Space-Time Rift

With all seventeen, step onto the altar at the Spear Pillar: "As the plate resonated, [you] sensed turbulence in space-time!… Activate the plate's power and enter the rift in space-time?" Inside the Space-Time Rift, Dialga and Palkia face you together in a level-50 double battle. Past them is a trainer from another world, Ruby or Sapphire depending on your character, who "crossed into this rift in spacetime to stop the Ultra Wormholes". Afterwards: "The Pokémon of time and space have ceased their strife now, and we've each caught one of them…"

4. Celestic Town and Giratina

Waji now senses "the same energy as inside the space-time rift coming from Celestic Town". The game checks your Pokédex in the ruins there: if Dialga and Palkia are both registered as caught, a new passage opens at the Spear Pillar, and another at Sendoff Spring. Waji: "While you were at Celestic Town, that altar suddenly changed, and a new passage appeared." Both passages lead to the Distortion World, where Giratina (level 80) waits.

The Distortion World can also be entered from a small islet on Route 129 in Hoenn, with no conditions, so you can reach Giratina without both Dialga and Palkia. Only Giratina and the Plates matter for what comes next.

Side story: the hidden ruins and the God-King

Cynthia is investigating newly found ruins off Route 210, which you can also reach from the Solaceon Ruins: "Perhaps if you gather all the tablets and set them into the empty slots in the underground ruins, something will be revealed." At one tablet Waji phones in a hint: "leap down from the northwest corner… remember to back up a bit and take a running jump."

With all seventeen Plates in your Bag, the wall of the ruins takes them ("[you] set the stone tablet into the wall of the ruins…!") and opens the Unown Ruins. There Cyrus, now calling himself the God-King, tells you to "command Palkia or Dialga and face me": "[you] must choose between the god of time and the god of space…!" The one you pick is a level-80 battle you cannot run from, and winning it also opens the Spear Pillar passage. Then Cyrus fights with Palkia, Dialga, two Arceus, Darkrai and Giratina, all level 100. Beaten, he casts you "into the world I once ruled", and you wake up in Twinleaf Town.

Your choice happens only once. The Dialga and Palkia in the ruins disappear as soon as you enter the ruins in Celestic Town, so if you have been there first (Cynthia studies a tablet there during the Sinnoh story) the God-King fight starts without the choice.

5. Arceus on the Mountain Top

Waji: "Dialga, who represents time, Palkia, who represents space, and Giratina, who represents the reverse world, are now all gathered in one Trainer. All 17 scattered shards have also been recovered." He tells you to meet Arceus at the summit, but the scene is not on Mt. Coronet. Go back to the Mountain Top above Team Rainbow Rocket's castle, where you beat them in the Hoenn post-game.

Hard requirement

Arceus appears only if you have battled Giratina and have all seventeen Plates: every Plate must still be in your Bag.

"[Your] seventeen Plates began to resonate with the space around them…" A way up opens, and at the top Arceus speaks: "I am the one you humans call the God of Creation, Arceus!… you have stopped the final catastrophe that would have burned all creation." The battle is level 80. Arceus joins you whether you catch it or win: "It is time I made good on My old promise." It warns of a final foe who "guides all things from within the bitter cold": "Go to Jiayuan City and seek out a certain someone." Jiayuan City is the untranslated name of Hearthome City.

6. Cogita and the Dawn of Hisui

Cogita lives in a house on the west side of Hearthome City. She will only talk to you with the seventeen Plates in your Bag. "Only those recognized by Arceus may pass freely through the rifts of space-time." She opens a space-time rift in her house and goes ahead to Hisui to gather "the last plate lost amid space and time".

  1. Prelude Beach. Rei and Akari mistake you for a drifter. Rei battles you, then flies you to the Deertrack Heights base camp. Rei is your guide in Hisui and flies you between the safe zones; some of his lines call him by his Chinese name, Mingyao.
  2. Firespit Island. Cogita is here: "Unlike that one lost in space-time, you were guided to this era by me." She sends you to the Diamond and Pearl Clans.
  3. The hot spring in the Alabaster Icelands. Adaman (Diamond Clan) is recovering from his wounds and Irida (Pearl Clan) is looking after both clans. Both clan treasures are lost. Talk to both, and "Diamond and Pearl both point to this spot at the foot of Mt. Coronet."
  4. Primeval Cave: Volo. Volo of the Ginkgo Guild asks whether you have seen "square stone plates carved with writing", then battles you once. When he recognises Arceus's power in you, he shows what he has become: "These rings have taken the place of the Red Chain. With them, I control Dialga and Palkia", and Giratina "bows at my feet". This is the final boss.

If you lose, "The powers of Dialga and Palkia resonated! [You were] sent back to before entering the Primordial Cave!" If you win, Volo breaks down: "Then what meaning is there in me keeping this Blank Plate? Take it. I am not worthy of it." "All the plates have been gathered! They're radiating a dazzling light!" Arceus appears to Volo and then to you: "Thou hast halted the calamity that would end the world, and gathered the eighteen fragments… Now let me witness this world together with you."

7. After the Dawn

  • Back at Prelude Beach, Rei reports that the space-time distortions are under control, and Cogita has heard what Volo was up to.
  • Now Adaman and Irida each sense a legendary in your party. Bring Dialga to Adaman and Palkia to Irida, and after a battle each hands over their clan's treasure: the Adamant Crystal and the Lustrous Globe.
  • Cogita in Hisui has one more legendary for a trainer who has caught Tornadus, Thundurus and Landorus: Enamorus.
  • Enamorus. Cogita on Firespit Island summons it herself ("Come forth!") once you have caught Tornadus, Thundurus and Landorus: a level-50 encounter. ROM
  • The God of Forms. Above Hisui's highlands, at the Moonbow Dome, a "God of Forms" shapes clay figures that rebuild themselves as fast as you beat them. The way up needs Cogita's Enamorus talk and Kitty's nameless stone: Kitty, the hack's lead artist, lives in the three-floor developer house on Steven's Island and wants dried fish, and the only Dried Fish in the game lies on Prelude Beach in Hisui. She calls it "at least a hundred years old! The same age as this stone!" and trades it for the stone. The Diamond Clan member in the highlands, who otherwise blames a landslide, then lets you head for the summit. In the fight "the nameless stone in your bag began to glow" and the clay figures crumble ("All things in this world were once nameless chaos…"); the god answers with the Rainbow Ring. Beaten, the god asks "take me with you… let me stay by your side and study your tactics", and you face it one last time as a level-70 Pokémon (the Enamorus species) that you can catch. ROM
  • After that, Cogita waits on Champion Island with a further mystery ("'They' may not be enemies…"). At her retreat off Sootopolis she runs the Dream Sky Gauntlet: four battles in a row, with healing between them.
  • Volo also appears in Hoenn's Sealed Chamber as a Ginkgo merchant selling rare goods, once you have read the Braille there.
  • A blue-haired passer-by offers, for 500,000, to send you "back to the recent past".
