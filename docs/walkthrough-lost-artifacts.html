Walkthrough · Part 3

Lost Artifacts questline

The story that names the hack: seventeen Plates scattered across two regions, a choice between time and space, Arceus, and a trip to ancient Hisui to face Volo. This is the last and hardest content in the game.

What you need before you start
  • Sinnoh League and the Ultra League cleared.
  • A strong level-100 team. Volo's final fight is six level-100 legendaries in a double battle, with Dynamax.
  • Note-taking: the game itself says "It might take a while, so take your own notes." The Plate list below saves you that.

1. Cynthia and the hidden ruins

Near Celestic Town, Cynthia investigates new ruins that appeared when the Ultra Wormholes started: "Perhaps if you gather all the tablets and set them into the empty slots in the underground ruins, something will be revealed." She has heard from Waji, the green-haired keeper of the ruins, that a tablet "records some kind of instruction". One tablet on the way tells you to "leap down from the northwest corner… remember to back up a bit and take a running jump."

3. The altar: Dialga or Palkia

"As the plate resonated, [you] sensed turbulence in space-time!… Activate the plate's power and enter the rift?" Inside, "[you] must choose between the god of time and the god of space." Whichever you choose is lent to you for the next fight; the other can be reached later at the Celestic ruins community. Both are level-80 encounters in the ROM.

4. The god-king

Cyrus has "mastered time and space… and reached the realm of the divine". You must fight him with the borrowed Dialga or Palkia: "You wish to challenge me, the god-king, without even a shred of divine power to match me?" His team is six level-100 Pokémon including both Dialga and Palkia.

On defeat he banishes you: "Cast him into the world I once ruled!… Wander that world of void for all eternity!" In the void world an old acquaintance from Petalburg Woods blocks the way home; beat them, and the Lake Guardians and Giratina return you: "Thank you for helping us free Palkia and Dialga… I will send you back to your world." Giratina has wrecked the spatial supports around the Spear Pillar, so leave before it collapses.

Waji afterwards: the same energy is now coming from Celestic Town. Go there, and when you return "that altar suddenly changed, and a new passage appeared."

5. Arceus on Mt. Coronet

Hard requirement

Waji only sends you up once "Dialga, Palkia and Giratina… are now all gathered in one Trainer" and "all 17 scattered shards have also been recovered." You need all three in your possession plus every Plate.

At the summit the Plates resonate and Arceus speaks: "I am the one you humans call the God of Creation, Arceus!… let Me first test your current strength!" The trial of Genesis is a battle against Arceus (level 80 in the ROM); losing gets "Unworthy! Our trial is absolute!" Winning: "We acknowledge you! Until the moment the world reaches its end, we shall lend you our strength!" Arceus points you onward: "Go to Jiayuan City and seek out a certain someone." That someone is Cogita.

6. Cogita and the Dawn of Hisui

Cogita, a "poet", tells the legend of Hisui and gives you a Hisuian Pokémon. Then: "Allow me to carry you back to that era, to gather the last plate lost amid space and time… Only those recognized by Arceus may pass freely through the rifts." She sends you to the Diamond and Pearl Clans.

  1. Prelude Beach. Rei and Akari meet you. Rei is your fast-travel NPC in Hisui ("Come find me when you need to go somewhere else") and his Rotom PC gives you box access.
  2. Deertrack Heights base camp. Rei explains: Volo tore open the rifts, was beaten at the Temple of Sinnoh and vanished; now the rifts spawn giant red-glowing Pokémon, which the game treats as Dynamax. Only the safe zones are reachable: Prelude Beach, Deertrack Heights, Firespit Island, the Snowfall hot spring, Coronet Highlands, the settlements and the Primeval Cave. Kleavor's rampage blocks Deertrack Path.
  3. Snowfall Hot Spring. Both clan leaders are here. Irida senses Palkia in your party and, after a battle, gives the Lustrous Globe. The wounded Adaman senses Dialga and, after a battle, gives the Adamant Crystal. Bring both legendaries. Both leaders point to the Coronet Highlands.
  4. Primeval Cave: Volo. He introduces himself as a Ginkgo Guild merchant hunting "square stone plates carved with writing" and battles you once to "take each other's measure". Then he recognises Arceus in your party and shows his "brand-new power": Hoopa's rings have replaced the Red Chain to control Dialga and Palkia in their Origin Formes, Giratina "bows at my feet", and he can Dynamax. This is the final boss.

Losing is not the end: "The powers of Dialga and Palkia resonated! [You] were sent back to before entering the Primeval Grotto!" Winning: "Take it. I am not worthy of holding it." He hands over the Blank Plate. "All the plates have been gathered! They shine with a dazzling light!" Arceus appears, redeems Volo, and gives itself to you: "The eighteen shards become one… Let me bear witness to the world together with thee." Arceus joins your party.

7. After the Dawn

  • Akari confirms the distortions are under control; Adaman and Irida invite you to the hot springs.
  • Back home, Cogita hints at a further mystery ("'They' may not be enemies…") and opens the Dream Sky Gauntlet at her retreat in Sootopolis: Cogita, Rei, Akari, Adaman, Irida and Volo in a row, healed between battles. She also grants Enamorus once you own the other three genies.
  • Volo also appears in Hoenn's Sealed Chamber as a Ginkgo merchant selling rare goods, once you have read the Braille there.
  • A blue-haired passer-by offers, for 500,000, to send you "back to the recent past", a New Game Plus style loop.
