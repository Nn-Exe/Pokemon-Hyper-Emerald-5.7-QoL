The post-game is longer than the main story. It runs in three chapters: the Hoenn post-game (Delta Episode, Ultra
Wormholes, Team Rainbow Rocket), World Championship Island, and the Sinnoh region. The Arceus story that follows
has [its own page](/guides/lost-artifacts/).

<Callout type="warn" title="Spoilers and sources">
  This page describes the whole story. The order of events and the quotes come from the game's own text; where a
  step is known only from community playthroughs of v5.5–5.6 it is marked <Tag type="warn">community</Tag>. Boss
  teams are read from the ROM.
</Callout>

## Chapter 1: Hoenn post-game

The Hoenn post-game is one chain of missions. Anabel on Steven's Island hands them out and you report back to her
after each one; the Devon Scout next to her says where the next Ultra Wormhole is. The order below comes from the
game's scripts: each step is gated on the one before it.

### Story order

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Interpol.</strong> After the Hall of Fame, Interpol agents Nanu and Anabel are waiting in Littleroot Town.</li>
  <li><strong>Steven's Island.</strong> The guard at the Pokémon League in Ever Grande City now lets you through the passage to a secret island. As you arrive, Wally calls from Petalburg City.</li>
  <li><strong>The Delta Episode.</strong> Wally in Petalburg, then Devon Corp. in Rustboro, a meteorite fragment in Granite Cave, the Mossdeep Space Center, Meteor Falls, the Sky Pillar and outer space (Deoxys). It ends with Wallace in Mossdeep. Details below.</li>
  <li><strong>Meteor Falls.</strong> Back on Steven's Island, Anabel reports trouble deep inside Meteor Falls, where Steven is investigating.</li>
  <li><strong>The Pacifidlog Ultra Wormhole.</strong> Anabel has found a new wormhole near Pacifidlog Town. Faba and Lusamine are behind it, and the other side is Cerulean Cave: Team Rocket and **Mewtwo**. Report to Anabel, who names the black beast: Necrozma.</li>
  <li><strong>The first Totem.</strong> Gladion asks for help at the Secret Base on Route 111. Anabel then explains the light crystals Necrozma scattered across Hoenn: each spawned a Totem Pokémon holding a Z-Crystal.</li>
  <li><strong>The Tapu trials.</strong> Nanu has found strange islands. Pass Tapu Bulu's trial with him, then find the other Kahunas: Hala in Dewford Town (Tapu Koko), Hapu in Slateport City (Tapu Fini) and Olivia in Mossdeep City (Tapu Lele). Report to Nanu for the Tapunium Z.</li>
  <li><strong>News of Faba, then the Scorched Slab wormhole.</strong> Talk to the Devon Scout on Steven's Island first: the wormhole in the Scorched Slab (Route 120; bring a way past whirlpools) will not let you in before his report. It leads to the Whirl Islands in Johto: Faba, Giovanni and Silver. Report to Anabel.</li>
  <li><strong>The Ultra League.</strong> Anabel sends you to the lobby, where Steven and the former Champions are waiting to train you. Report to Anabel, with Gold and Kris.</li>
  <li><strong>Abnormal weather.</strong> The Devon Scout links the fluctuations to abnormal weather: **Terra Cave** (drought: Maxie and Groudon) or **Marine Cave** (downpour: Archie and Kyogre). Either one moves the story on. Report to Anabel.</li>
  <li><strong>Mt. Pyre's summit wormhole</strong> leads to the Spear Pillar (Cyrus and Team Galactic) and the Distortion World, where the Lake Guardians thank you for freeing Dialga and Palkia and send you home. Report to Anabel.</li>
  <li><strong>Shoal Cave's icy room</strong> leads to the Giant Chasm: Team Plasma, Ghetsis and Kyurem. Report to Anabel.</li>
  <li><strong>The Route 116 wormhole</strong> leads to Allearth Forest and Lake: Team Flare, Paul and Yveltal. Report to Anabel.</li>
  <li><strong>The Mirage Tower.</strong> Faba is seen alone near the tower in the Route 111 desert, and Team Flare, Alain and Paul ambush you there. Anabel then learns that Faba has joined the leaders of the old villain teams as **Team Rainbow Rocket**.</li>
  <li><strong>Find Red.</strong> The last wormholes are deep in Meteor Falls and Granite Cave. Red is on Mt. Silver. Report to Anabel, who calls a war council of the Champions.</li>
  <li><strong>Team Rainbow Rocket.</strong> Collect all **18 Z-Crystals** and go to Pacifidlog Town. The crystals open the way to the Rainbow Castle, and the fight ends on the Mountain Top above it. Then Steven is waiting on Steven's Island.</li>
</ol>

### After the Hall of Fame

- Your father gives **Ticket 1** for the Battle Frontier; Birch upgrades the Pokédex; trading is unlocked.
- The Day-Care Man on Route 117 now hands over the <Item name="Oval Charm" />.
- Team Magma's "strongest man" **Yanshan** waits in the old Magma Hideout and teaches **V-create** when beaten.
  Team Aqua's "strongest empress" waits in the Aqua Hideout. Both are optional.

### Delta Episode: Zinnia and the meteorite

<ol class="steps">
  <li><strong>Petalburg City.</strong> Wally is being attacked by a Team Magma remnant who wants his Mega Bracelet. Drive her off. Steven then calls with an emergency.</li>
  <li><strong>Devon Corp., Rustboro.</strong> President Stone explains the meteoroid heading for the planet and Devon's **Infinity Energy**: Plan S uses that energy to push the meteoroid off course; Plan D, the "Destroyer Meteor", is the fallback. You are sent for a fragment of meteorite.</li>

### Steven's Island

The hub for everything that follows. Anabel (International Police) runs the Ultra Wormhole survey from the
conference room, with the Devon Scout beside her.

- **The Ultra League.** When Anabel sends you to the lobby, Champions from every region are there, and you beat
  them one after another: Blue, Lance, Alder, Iris, Diantha, Elio and Selene, Gladion, the Kalos champion X, N,
  Hilda, Black, Lucas, Serena and more. All are level 100; teams are in the
  [Trainer Battles](/wiki/trainers/) index.
- An NPC on the right side of the island gives <Item name="Beast Ball" label="Beast Balls" />. The villa NPC gives
  the <Item name="Old Sea Map" /> for **Mew** on Faraway Island (sail from Lilycove). **Selene** on the right of
  the island re-awakens any Totem you beat without collecting its Z-Crystal. A "MoeMoe" attendant gives your lead
  Pokémon perfect IVs.
- **Yanshan** (optional, in the three-floor developer house): Team Magma's "Dark King" offers "the ultimate
  battle beyond despair" and lends you a Dynamax Band for it. Win and he gives you the
  <Item name="Key Stone" /> and takes the band back; lose and he just takes the band back.
  <Tag type="accent">ROM</Tag>
- **Kitty** (same house), the hack's lead artist, restores your team and wants dried fish: bring her the
  <Item name="Dried Fish" /> from Prelude Beach in Hisui for the **nameless stone**, the key to the God of Forms
  (see the [Lost Artifacts](/guides/lost-artifacts/#7-after-the-dawn) page). **Huang Banxian** gives one free
  Egg, then sells them for 10,000. <Tag type="accent">ROM</Tag>
- Grinding spot <Tag type="warn">community</Tag>: the NPC at the mansion entrance uses level 75–80 Wobbuffet,
  Blissey, Chansey and Lickitung. Bring Fighting types.

### Anabel's missions in detail

- **Pacifidlog.** Faba (level 65, a Double Battle with Lusamine) is the first boss of the Ultra Wormhole survey.
  Cerulean Cave has the Team Rocket executives Archer, Ariana and Proton, then <Mon name="Mewtwo" /> at level 50.
  A later Armored Mewtwo event exists ("The armor restraining Mewtwo has been removed… Poké Balls will work
  again!").
- **Groudon and Kyogre.** In Terra Cave, Maxie fights with a level-100 team and Primal Groudon, then
  <Mon name="Groudon" /> is a level-70 encounter. In Marine Cave, Archie does the same with <Mon name="Kyogre" />.
  Both encounters are scripted battles found in the ROM.
- **Spear Pillar and the Distortion World.** Cyrus fights you with rental Pokémon; you cannot use your own.
  <Mon name="Giratina" /> (level 80, holding a Griseous Orb) is in the Distortion World. If you leave it there,
  you can still battle it later from Sinnoh.
- **Giant Chasm.** Two Ghetsis fights (doubles, then singles). Rewards: the <Item name="DNA Splicers" /> and a
  second Cosmog. <Mon name="Kyurem" /> is here at level 50.
- **Allearth Forest and Lake** (the Kalos forest): Lysandre with Xerneas and Yveltal on his team, and the two
  legendaries themselves at level 50. Follow the capital-letter path in the Magic Woods
  <Tag type="warn">community</Tag>: up, right, left, down, down, left, left, left, right, right, down, down.
- **Mt. Silver.** Two battles with Red. The game hints that the first is not the real one: "It feels as if
  something deep within Mt. Silver is resonating…! A Pressure very much like Red's own…!"

<Callout type="info" title="No PC in six late dungeons">
  The patch's PC-anywhere button is switched off in Rainbow Castle, Allearth Forest, Giant Chasm, Spear Pillar,
  the Distortion World and Mt. Silver, to keep them a challenge. A PC that stands in the area still works, and so
  do your registered key items.
</Callout>

### Other wormholes and side trips

None of these is part of Anabel's chain. You need **Cosmog or its evolutions in the party** for the spatial gates,
or the **Ultra Suit** from Lusamine's experiment.

- **Mt. Pyre**: an Ultra Recon researcher gives <Mon name="Poipole" /> (evolves by learning Dragon Pulse);
  <Mon name="Cresselia" /> (level 50) is on Mt. Pyre.
- **New Mauville**: <Mon name="Genesect" />, level 50.
- **Route 116 (Nebel Cave / Timeless Woods)**: <Mon name="Volcanion" /> (level 50) and an
  <Mon name="Floette (Eternal Flower)" label="Eternal Flower Floette" />.
- **Icirrus City and Dragonspiral Tower**: <Mon name="Zekrom" /> and <Mon name="Reshiram" />, level 50.
- **Mysterious island**: Lyra hands you a "ferry ticket to Johto" that is really for an island with the legendary
  birds and the Johto cast (Silver, Gold, Ethan, Kris). Bird battles lead to <Mon name="Ho-Oh" /> at the summit
  and a rampaging <Mon name="Shadow Lugia" />; keep exploring the island cave after Lapras ("It seems Lapras is
  not the master of this island…"). Articuno and Zapdos are scripted at Navel Rock in the ROM.
- **The Ultra Null Space**: Faba again, now with Solgaleo, Lunala, Silvally, Hoopa, Nihilego and Guzzlord. He
  boasts of collecting two more Cosmog from parallel dimensions. Necrozma is "trapped in a prison of lightning".

### Team Rainbow Rocket

Anabel's war council follows the Mt. Silver report: "Have you collected all 18 Z-Crystals? If you did, then go to
Pacifidlog Town." Eighteen **Totem Pokémon** (level 80, each holding its type's Z-Crystal) sit in hidden
base-style spots around Hoenn; the [Z-Crystals table](/guides/mega-stones-key-items/#z-crystals) lists them. With
all 18: "The 18 Z-Crystals let out a mighty surge…! A suspicious opening seems to have appeared…!"

The opening leads to Team Rainbow Rocket's base, the **Rainbow Castle**. Its bosses are villains from parallel
worlds: Giovanni, Lysandre, Cyrus, an alternate Maxie who "turned my entire world into land", an alternate Archie
whose world "ended up swallowed entirely by the sea", Ghetsis with Kyurem and the DNA Splicers, and Faba. Admins
Jupiter, Zinzolin, Xerosic, Shelly and Tabitha fight in level-88 doubles. <Tag type="warn">community</Tag>: on the
harder modes Giovanni must be beaten within ten turns, and losing revives you at the base.

The arc ends on the **Mountain Top** above the castle, where Giovanni uses the "Spear of Heaven" and the assembled
Champions rally you ("Believe in the bond between you and your Pokémon, the world will not disappear that
easily!"). Afterwards the original developer's note plays: "You've made it through the Ultra League and Team
Rainbow Rocket! Unfortunately, that's all the story progress there is for now." Everything below was added in
later versions. Go back to Steven's Island, where Steven is waiting.

## Chapter 2: World Championship Island

- **Getting there.** Take the **ferry from Lilycove City**: Champion Island joins its list once you hold the World
  Championship ticket. **Scott**, in his house at the Battle Frontier, gives it with his PWT invitation once you
  have your first Silver Symbol from any facility (for example Salon Maiden Anabel, the 35th battle of a Battle
  Tower Singles streak). Its story scene only starts once Team Rainbow Rocket is beaten. Later the **Snowpoint
  icebreaker** in Sinnoh also goes straight to the island.
- **Paul and Waji.** Paul, now out of Team Rainbow Rocket, battles you as you arrive. Then Waji, whom you last met
  in Petalburg Woods, gives you the **boat ticket to Canalave City**: "Take it to the harbor of Lilycove City and
  show it to the sailor… If you can't find them, go to the Spear Pillar. Someone there will guide you."
- **Dynamax Band.** Leon was invited by Steven but stayed to fight the Darkest Day; he explains Eternatus "escaped
  through a spatial distortion" into an Ultra Space in Hoenn and gives the <Item name="Dynamax Band" />. If you
  miss him, Sonia in his room (the small house below the Pokémon Center) gives one.
- **Eternatus.** Leon is later found wounded in Ultra Space; you subdue <Mon name="Eternatus" /> alone (level 50
  scripted encounter). Leon then offers Dynamax rematches at a Wishing Star power spot; the Max Soup NPC gives
  your lead Pokémon the Gigantamax factor.
- **Rules.** The tournament desk explains the format: standard doubles, bring six choose four, no duplicate
  species or items, no mythicals. In v5.7 the tournament itself is not open; the island hosts the Galar gym
  leaders (Bea, Allister, Avery, Klara at level 100 doubles), Koga, Paul and others.
- **Koga's mark.** Beat Koga and he marks you. That unlocks the **ninja village** hidden through the Route 102
  forest: "Please come with only a single Greninja in tow." Win the trial and your Greninja gains Battle Bond,
  becoming <Mon name="Ash-Greninja" />. Once you own one, Ash visits Route 101 for a tag battle.
- **Hyper Training.** A trainer on the island Hyper Trains your Pokémon; with the patch the EV-IV Display shows 31
  for a trained stat and the stats rise the moment he finishes.
- After the Sinnoh League every TM is sold here, and it is the place to farm PP Ups
  <Tag type="warn">community</Tag>.

## Chapter 3: Sinnoh

### Arrival

Sail from Lilycove to **Canalave City**. Lucas meets you: "Every town in Sinnoh has Ride Pokémon that can fly you
around. But since you're new here, you should visit the towns first." Fly points unlock as you visit. The Ride
Pokémon summoner is in the Team Galactic warehouse in Veilstone, which also holds an HM. As Hoenn Champion you may
Surf anywhere at once.

<Callout title="The Sinnoh Map">
  The patch hands you a <Item name="Sinnoh Map" /> key item. Open it (or register it to SELECT), move the red box
  over a town whose courier you have already used and press <Key>A</Key> to fly there. In Hisui it shows Hisui.
</Callout>

### Sinnoh League

- Requirements: eight Sinnoh badges, **exactly six Pokémon**, no eggs.
- Victory Road: Lucas and Barry meet you at the end; Barry battles, Lucas gives an item. Scripted level-80
  pseudo-legendaries (Hydreigon, Salamence, Metagross, Dragonite) live inside.
- Format: "standard Double Battle rules… The Elite Four await behind the four doors on either side. You may
  challenge them in any order you like." A staff member heals you between fights and a monitor shows each
  opponent's team. Aaron (Bug), Bertha (Ground), Flint (Fire) and Lucian (Psychic) are followed by **Cynthia in
  serious mode**. The ROM stores only placeholders for these fights; the teams are built at battle time.
- After the League: <Mon name="Heatran" /> in a new Mt. Coronet opening reached from Snowpoint via Route 216
  (level 30 in the ROM); every TM goes on sale at the Championship island.
