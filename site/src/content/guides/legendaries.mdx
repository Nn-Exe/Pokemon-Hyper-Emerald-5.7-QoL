<Callout type="plain" title="Reading the entries">
  <Tag type="accent">ROM</Tag> means the encounter (species and level) was found in the game's map scripts for v5.7
  bugfix 2. <Tag type="warn">community</Tag> means the trigger or route comes from player guides for v5.5–5.6 and
  has not been re-verified in v5.7. Ultra Beasts need **Beast Balls** (the NPC right of Steven's villa). Bring
  plenty of Max Repels into Ultra caves. Some legendaries cannot be used in gym or league battles in the harder
  modes.
</Callout>

In game, the Quest Log's **Legends** chapter ticks each one off as you catch it and names where it is once you
have seen it. Every fixed wild battle in the ROM, legendary or not, is in the
[Scripted Encounters](/wiki/scripted-encounters/) index.

## Shiny hunting them

- The hack uses odds of 1/8192 per try. Your PC starts with a <Item name="Shiny Charm" />.
- Save in front of a scripted encounter and reset if it is not shiny. With the patch, a shiny Pokémon's battle box
  is **gold** from the moment it appears, so you can tell before the sprite finishes loading.
- For ordinary wild Pokémon, a DexNav search adds shiny rolls as the species' Search Level rises (about 0.33% at
  Search Level 255 and 0.61% at 999, multiplied by five with the Shiny Charm), with extra rolls on the 50th and
  100th encounter of a chain.
- A few side-content Pokémon are always shiny: the hidden Snivy, Litten, Grookey and Scorbunny battles, the
  Drifloon gift and the cursed statue's Gengar. The Quest Log marks them with a red star.
