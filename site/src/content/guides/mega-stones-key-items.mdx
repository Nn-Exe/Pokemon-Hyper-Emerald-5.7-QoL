## Mega Stones

Read from the game's own scripts (v5.7 bugfix 2). A Mega Stone lies on the ground as a small round stone of
many colours, not as a Poké Ball: each picture shows the map around one, with its tile framed. Click a
picture to enlarge it. Five are handed over instead, and the list says by whom. The two orbs behind Primal
Reversion and Rayquaza's Final Meteor are in the list too.

Professor Elm on Steven's Island does not hand out a stone for showing him the Kanto starters' Mega Stones in
v5.7: the script for it is still in the game, but nothing runs it, and he only says he is busy.

## Z-Crystals

Read from the game's scripts. Each of the eighteen type Z-Crystals belongs to a **Totem Pokémon**, Lv 80, that
waits in a Secret Base room. A room has three or four ways in: the Secret Base spots pictured for it below (a
big tree, a shrub or an indent in a rock wall). Use Secret Power on any one of them and go in.

- The Totems appear once you have reported back to Anabel on Steven's Island from your first journey through
  an Ultra Wormhole. Gladion is then back at the big tree on Route 111: the first Totem, Wishiwashi, is inside
  it, and the other seventeen do not show until it is beaten.
- The room asks "Want to battle the Totem?". The game refuses ("Can't use some moves!") while a Pokémon in
  your party knows Destiny Bond, Perish Song, Pain Split or Leech Seed.
- Win, and the crystal is handed to you; the Totem does not come back. If you won and the crystal is not in
  your Bag, Selene on Steven's Island wakes that Totem again.

## The Plates

Read from the game's scripts: fifteen lie in item balls (one ball holds two), a man gives you the Icicle Plate,
and the Blank and Legend Plates come at the very end of the Hisui story. Waji's riddle for each one and the
rest of the questline are on the [Lost Artifacts page](/guides/lost-artifacts/#2-the-17-plates).

## HMs

Cut (Rustboro), Flash (Granite Cave miner), Rock Smash (Mauville), Strength, Surf (Wally's father after Norman),
Fly, Dive, Waterfall (after Rayquaza calms the weather). With the patch, owning the HM and its badge is enough:
Cut, Strength, Rock Smash, Surf, Waterfall and Dive work even when nobody in your party can learn them, and Flash
is offered in the party menu inside a dark cave. In Sinnoh an HM is stored in the Team Galactic warehouse, and
Rock Climb is available to Sinnoh trainers and via the ride gear.
