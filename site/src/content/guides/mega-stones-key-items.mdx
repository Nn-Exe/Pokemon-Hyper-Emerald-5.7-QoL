In game, the Quest Log's **Key Items** chapter lists the 55 key items in story order, names each one from the start
and says where it comes from.

## Z-Crystals

All eighteen type Z-Crystals are held by **Totem Pokémon** at level 80, each in a hidden base-style spot in Hoenn.
The species and crystals are read from the ROM; the route hints are from community guides. If you beat a Totem
without getting its crystal, Selene on Steven's Island can re-awaken it. Collecting all 18 is required to reach
Team Rainbow Rocket's castle and Necrozma.

## HMs

Cut (Rustboro), Flash (Granite Cave miner), Rock Smash (Mauville), Strength, Surf (Wally's father after Norman),
Fly, Dive, Waterfall (after Rayquaza calms the weather). With the patch, owning the HM and its badge is enough:
Cut, Strength, Rock Smash, Surf, Waterfall and Dive work even when nobody in your party can learn them, and Flash
is offered in the party menu inside a dark cave. In Sinnoh an HM is stored in the Team Galactic warehouse, and
Rock Climb is available to Sinnoh trainers and via the ride gear.
