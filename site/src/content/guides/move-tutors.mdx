The game has a list of 127 tutor moves, and **41 tutors** teach every one of them. Some teach for free, as often
as you like. Sixteen want ¥5,000 a move once you have done them a favour, four take coloured Shards, and two in
the Battle Frontier price their moves in Battle Points. Eight more belong to the story or to one Pokémon.

<Callout type="info" title="Where this list comes from">
  Each tutor was found and followed through the scripts of the v2.0 game: the moves come from the list the script
  hands to the game, the price from what it takes, and the favour from the checks it makes before the menu opens.
  Chase's partner moves are taught another way, by a routine of the hack's own, and were read from it.
  Each picture shows the map around the tutor, with their tile framed. Click a picture to enlarge it.
</Callout>

A Pokémon can learn a tutor move only if the move is on that species' own list. The party screen a tutor opens
marks each Pokémon as able to learn it or not, and every [Pokédex](/wiki/pokedex/) page now lists the tutor moves
that species can learn, with the cheapest way to get each one. To look a move up, go to
[Who teaches each move](#who-teaches-each-move).

## Free tutors

Most of these teach a single move and never run out. The two menus (Ever Grande City and Champion
Island) are free too.

## Tutors who ask a favour first

Each of these wants something first: an item, a delivery or a task. After that they teach for ¥5,000 a move.
You need ¥5,000 on you to open the menu, and the money is taken only once a Pokémon has learned the move:
change your mind on the party screen and you keep it.

<TutorList group="favour" />

## Shard tutors

Each Shard tutor stands in two places with the same moves. The Shards are taken **before** the menu opens, so
cancelling after that loses them.

<TutorList group="shards" />

## Battle Frontier tutors

The old Beauty and the old Swimmer sit side by side in the Battle Frontier's Lounge 7, ten moves each.

<Callout type="warn" title="The Battle Points are never taken">
  Each move shows a Battle Point price, and you need that many Battle Points to choose it, but they stay in your
  pocket: the step that should take them lost the line that does it in the hack's own script (the unpatched
  hack has the same bytes). Tested in the v2.0 game: 100 Battle Points before a lesson, 100 after.
  The same slip sends you back to the wrong menu: after a lesson the menu that comes back is always the
  Swimmer's, so talk to the Beauty again for hers.
</Callout>

"not compatible".

## The Move Relearner

The patch adds **Moves** to the party menu: pick a Pokémon, choose Moves, and it can relearn a move for free,
anywhere. The list holds the level-up moves up to its current level and, since v2.0, its egg moves too. The
game's own Move Relearners, in Fallarbor Town and in Pastoria City, open the same screen for a Heart Scale.
Tutor moves are not on that list: a Pokémon that forgets one has to go back to a tutor.
