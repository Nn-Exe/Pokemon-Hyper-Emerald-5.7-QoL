The Journal follows the story. This page is the rest: every optional battle, gift and scene found by reading the
game's scripts. In game, the Quest Log's **Side Content** chapter ticks most of these off as you do them, with a
red star on the ones whose Pokémon is always shiny.

<Callout type="info" title="Where this list comes from">
  Each row was read from the scripts of v5.7 bugfix 2, not from a wiki, so it lists what the game can actually do.
  Levels are the ones the script sets.
</Callout>

## Gift Pokémon

<SideContent group="Gift Pokémon" />

Early in the game: Professor Oak offers a <Mon name="Let's Go Pikachu" /> or <Mon name="Let's Go Eevee" /> in
Petalburg Woods, and an NPC on Route 104 gives a <Mon name="Wimpod" />. See
[Game Mechanics](/guides/mechanics/#starters-and-early-gifts).

## Other regions' starters

<SideContent group="Other regions' starters" />

The hidden <Mon name="Snivy" />, <Mon name="Litten" />, <Mon name="Grookey" /> and <Mon name="Scorbunny" /> are
always shiny.
