## Littleroot Town

<Stage>Start</Stage>

- **Set the clock = pick your difficulty.** It cannot be changed later. For the hidden Lunatic mode, use the
  notebook by the PC ten times first. See [Game Mechanics](/guides/mechanics/#difficulty-modes).
- Your PC already holds a <Item name="Shiny Charm" />, an <Item name="EV-IV Display" /> and Power items.
- Route 101: press <Key>▶</Key> on the starter screen to scroll through all 24 starters. The Route 101
  <Mon name="Zigzagoon" /> carry the Ability Pill and Ability Patch as held items; come back with Thief later.
- Route 103 rival battle, then the Pokédex and Poké Balls as usual. <Mon name="Ralts" /> on Route 102 have
  Synchronize. Once you have the Pokédex, <Key>R</Key> opens the DexNav.

## Petalburg, Petalburg Woods, Rustboro

<Stage>Badge 1 · cap Lv 18</Stage>

- Petalburg Gym is locked until you have four badges, as in vanilla. Wally's catching tutorial happens here.
- **Petalburg Woods:** Professor Oak appears and offers a <Mon name="Let's Go Pikachu" /> or
  <Mon name="Let's Go Eevee" />. On Route 104 an NPC gives a <Mon name="Wimpod" />.
  <Item name="Scizorite" /> is hidden in the woods.
- **Rustboro:** the Trainer's School teacher changes natures with Mints (no quiz, with the patch). The house
  top-right of the gym trades a <Mon name="Zorua" />. Devon Corp 2F has <Item name="Pidgeotite" />;
  <Mon name="Magearna" /> waits behind the receptionist's door much later in the game.

### Gym 1: Roxanne (Rock)

<Team id={265} />

Six Pokémon at level 18 with hazards (Stealth Rock, Sticky Web, Toxic Spikes) and Eviolite fossils. Bring Grass
or Water and a way to remove hazards; Rock Tomb is your reward.

- Devon parcel errand as vanilla: Rusturf Tunnel, then Mr. Stone gives the PokéNav and the letter for Steven.

## Dewford, Granite Cave, Slateport

<Stage>Badge 2 · cap Lv 23</Stage>

- Granite Cave: deliver the letter to Steven; a miner gives Flash. The <Item name="Earth Plate" /> is in a side
  cave at the deepest part of 1F (you can only use it much later).

### Gym 2: Brawly (Fighting)

<Team id={266} />

Hisuian Sneasler and Lucario make this a mixed Fighting/Poison and Steel wall. Flying and Psychic moves, and a
Fairy type if you have one, carry the fight.

- Slateport: the Contest Hall and Museum play out as vanilla; the shipyard's second floor had a freeze in earlier
  releases of the hack itself, fixed in its v5.7 bugfix builds.
- Route 110 rival battle. <Item name="Manectite" /> is hidden along the Cycling Road side.

## Mauville

<Stage>Badge 3 · cap Lv 28</Stage>

- The house right of the Pokémon Center gives a bike; with the patch you get the other one too, and can switch
  from the Bag or the SELECT ring.
- Wally battles you at the gym entrance with a Kirlia.

### Gym 3: Wattson (Electric)

<Team id={267} />

Six level-28 Electric types including Rotom and Magnezone. Ground types are immune to most of it; Pincurchin and
Rotom will still hurt. Wattson is a known wall on the harder modes.

### Side quest: the Team Rocket hideout under the Game Corner

The Skitty errand in Mauville leads to a hidden **Team Rocket base beneath the Game Corner**. Two door passwords
are needed, typed in capitals: `ARCHER` on the first floor, then `ROCKET` for the second-floor check, answered
No / Yes / No. Inside you fight a Rocket researcher with a level-40 "DNA" Eevee that transforms, and you can catch
the special **DNA Eevee** afterwards (it is treated as a 3-badge Pokémon for obedience). The base also holds the
<Item name="Iron Plate" /> and <Item name="Dread Plate" />. The Eevee misbehaves and loses HP each turn until you
receive an item that triggers automatically at the exit of Victory Road later in the story.

  Community reports for v5.5–5.7 (pokeharbor comments, 9game); the plate placement is confirmed by the Sinnoh plate
  hints in the game text.
</p>

## Route 111 to Fallarbor, Meteor Falls, Mt. Chimney

<Stage>Badge 4 · cap Lv 37</Stage>

- Fallarbor: the house east of town holds the "trade" NPC (answer No to evolve trade-evolution Pokémon).
  <Item name="Galladite" /> is in Professor Cozmo's house.
- Meteor Falls: Team Magma and Aqua fight over the meteorite as vanilla. <Item name="Salamencite" /> is on 1F and
  <Item name="Aerodactylite" /> deeper in. A locked Draconid cave here matters in the post-game (Draco Plate,
  Steven, Cosmog).
- Mt. Chimney: cable car up, then Maxie with Tabitha and Courtney. Zinnia already fights here as a Team Magma
  member. Jagged Pass has <Item name="Tyranitarite" />.

<Team id={602} />

### Gym 4: Flannery (Fire)

<Team id={268} />

A Typhlosion, Arcanine, Charizard and Cinderace at level 37. Water and Rock coverage, and a Rotom answer, are
enough. Go-Goggles for the desert follow.

## Petalburg again

<Stage>Badge 5 · cap Lv 45</Stage>

### Gym 5: Norman (Normal)

<Team id={269} />

Six level-45 Pokémon with Snorlax, Ursaluna and a Hisuian Zoroark. Fighting types are the answer to everything
except the Zoroark; watch for its Illusion.

<Callout title="Mega Bracelet">
  After the badge, go home to Littleroot and talk to your mother. She gives the <Item name="Mega Bracelet" />, a
  gift from Devon your father held back. Wally's father gives Surf as usual.
</Callout>

## Route 118, Weather Institute, Fortree

<Stage>Badge 6 · cap Lv 51</Stage>

- Weather Institute: Team Aqua, Shelly, and Castform as in vanilla. The rival fights you on Route 119 afterwards
  with a full team of six.
- Fortree: the trainer behind the Pokémon Center hands over the <Item name="Z-Power Ring" label="Z-Ring" /> when
  beaten. The Pokémon Center also has a trade-evolution NPC and a tutor for the Let's Go partner moves.
  <Item name="Garchompite" /> is in town.

### Gym 6: Winona (Flying)

<Team id={270} />

Celesteela and Dragonite raise the ceiling to level 51. Ice and Rock moves, and Electric for the rest.

## Routes 120–121, Mt. Pyre, Lilycove, Mossdeep

<Stage>Badge 7 · cap Lv 57</Stage>

- Route 120: Scorched Slab holds <Item name="Charizardite X" />. Kecleon ambushes on 119/120 as vanilla.
- Mt. Pyre: <Item name="Gengarite" /> lies between two of the four stones in the foggy area. The wild Pokémon on
  the Route 122 side carry evolution stones to steal.
- Lilycove: rival battle with six Pokémon, then the Team Aqua hideout (Matt). The Department Store sells mints.
- Mossdeep: the gym is blocked by an NPC until you finish a short Shoal Cave quest (battle Hugh and Team Plasma
  in the icy room). <Item name="Slowbronite" /> is on Shoal Cave 1F; <Item name="Pinsirite" /> on the left side
  of Mossdeep.

### Gym 7: Tate & Liza (Psychic, Double Battle)

<Team id={271} />

Two Indeedee run Psychic Terrain support for Metagross, Gallade and Gardevoir. Dark and Ghost attackers, or a
Steel wall, do well; spread moves earn their keep.

## Seafloor Cavern, Sootopolis, Sky Pillar

<Stage>Badge 8 · cap Lv 62</Stage>

- Space Center, Seafloor Cavern and Archie's Kyogre play out as vanilla. Archie's team is below.
- Sootopolis: Groudon and Kyogre clash; Steven sends you to the Cave of Origin, then Wallace to the Sky Pillar on
  Route 131. The Sky Pillar needs the <Item name="Mach Bike" />. <Mon name="Rayquaza" /> calms the two and you
  receive Waterfall.

<Team id={34} />

### Gym 8: Juan (Water)

<Team id={272} />

Level 62 with Kingdra, Swampert and Hisuian Basculegion. Grass moves for Swampert and Politoed, Electric for the
rest; Kingdra only fears Dragon and Fairy.

## Victory Road and Ever Grande

<Stage>Elite Four · Level 70 · Double Battles</Stage>

- Victory Road needs every HM (with the patch, owning the HMs is enough). Wally fights you inside with five
  Pokémon in the 75–80 range. At the exit, if you caught the DNA Eevee, the researcher from the Mauville lab gives
  you the <Item name="Core Ring" />: held by that Eevee, it stops the backlash it takes after attacking.
- The Hoenn League in this hack is fought in **Double Battles at level 70**. Bring exactly six Pokémon. Status
  moves do not work on the Elite Four.

## After the credits

You wake up at home. Your father gives a ticket for the Battle Frontier and Professor Birch upgrades the Pokédex.
Interpol agents Nanu and Anabel are waiting in Littleroot Town. After them, the guard at the Pokémon League in
Ever Grande City lets you through to Steven's Island, and as you arrive Wally calls: a Team Magma remnant is
attacking him in Petalburg. That starts the post-game; continue with
[Post-game: Sinnoh & Beyond](/guides/walkthrough-postgame/).
