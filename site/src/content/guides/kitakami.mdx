Kitakami is an optional post-game area added in v2.0: **Mossui Town**, a valley of red maples with a festival
market, Kitakami Hall and Loyalty Plaza. It holds the Teal Mask story in eight chapters with Carmine, Kieran and
Ogerpon, then Mochi Mayhem with your friends from Paldea and Pecharunt. Its battles are level 70 to 82.

## Getting there

- Finish **Area Zero's story** first (the [Area Zero guide](/guides/area-zero/)): once Jacq has gone back in, **Briar**
  stands beside the black hole in **Mossdeep City**, on the grass below the Space Center.
- Talk to her and say Yes: her bus takes you to **Mossui Town's bus stop**. She waits there to take you back to
  Mossdeep whenever you like.
- **Kitakami Hall** is in the north-west of the town, **Loyalty Plaza** in its grove in the north-east, the market
  street down the middle, Carmine and Kieran's house on the west and the **Pokémon Center** on the east.

## The Teal Mask

Every story battle here is fought with no white-out: lose and you are healed, and you can try again on the spot.

1. **Carmine** stops you as you step off the bus and battles you. Kieran runs up, says sorry for his sister and
   sends you to Kitakami Hall.

   <Team id={1358} />

2. **Kitakami Hall**, the Mask Hall: the Loyal Three's masks stand in glass cases, and the caretaker before the
   stage asks you to read the legend's **three signboards** (notice boards under a red roof).
3. **The signboards**: one among the rice fields in the west, one among those in the east, one at the north end of
   the avenue, west of Loyalty Plaza. Kieran waits by each in turn; read it with him there.
4. **Loyalty Plaza**: Kieran tells you about the ogre before the Loyal Three's stone statues. Evening falls, and
   the **Festival of Masks** begins.
5. **The festival**: Carmine waits at the market gate. Ogerpon runs into you in the crowd and drops the Teal Mask.
   From now on the stalls are shops (below).
6. **Night**: at Carmine and Kieran's house their grandfather tells the true story of the ogre. The mask is mended
   overnight, then gone.
7. **Kieran** waits at Loyalty Plaza with the mask and battles you. The Loyal Three wake, Ogerpon comes, and the
   three are driven off with her masks.

   <Team id={1359} />

8. **The hunt and the finale**: <Mon name="Okidogi" />, <Mon name="Munkidori" /> and <Mon name="Fezandipiti" />
   each hide somewhere in the town; beat each for its mask (no Poké Balls). Then Kieran battles you for Ogerpon, she
   fights you in each of her four masks, and you catch her. Your team is healed before each mask's battle and
   before the catch.

   <Team id={1360} />

After the story **Kieran's grandfather**, at home, changes the mask of the first <Mon name="Ogerpon" /> in your
party (Teal, Wellspring, Hearthflame or Cornerstone), and the Loyal Three can be caught where they hide.

## Mochi Mayhem

<div class="shot-grid cols-2">
  <Clip slug="kieran-night-market" />
</div>

After the Teal Mask, **Briar** has a letter from Kieran, in Mossdeep City or at Mossui's bus stop (the mochi stall's
vendor tells you she was looking for you). Mossui is under a spell of mochi, and it stays night there until it is
over:

- In the market, **Kieran** meets you with **Arven**, **Nemona** and **Penny**; Carmine will not stop dancing.
  Nemona runs off for mochi.
- Wake the apple farmer by the paddies and the mother on the street by talking to them, then Kieran's grandparents
  in front of Kitakami Hall, then Arven and Penny in the market. A peach-shaped Pokémon shows itself over the market
  and flies off.
- At **Loyalty Plaza** Nemona battles you. Then <Mon name="Pecharunt" /> battles you (Lv 82, no Poké Balls): beat it
  and it is yours. With a full party it goes to the PC.
- The morning after, Kieran and Carmine see you off at the bus stop.
