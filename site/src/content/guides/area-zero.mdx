Area Zero is an optional post-game area added in v1.9: Paldea's Great Crater, reached through a black hole in
Mossdeep City once Team Rainbow Rocket is beaten. It has a short story of its own, the twenty Paradox Pokémon, and
the Gen 9 legends to catch when the story is over. Everything here is level 63 to 80.

<Callout type="info" title="Where this page comes from">
  Read from the v1.9 game: the area's scripts (who says what, what each step needs, the levels they set), its wild
  encounter tables and the trainers' teams. The Journal follows the story too: seven rows tagged optional in its
  Post-game chapter, listed on the [Journal page](/guides/journal/).
</Callout>

## Getting there

- **Jacq**, a teacher from Paldea's academy, stands in **Mossdeep City** from the start of the game, on the grass
  by the sea below the Space Center (down the little stairs). He has brought three students to Hoenn on a field
  study: Arven, Nemona and Penny.
- Once **Team Rainbow Rocket** is beaten, a **black hole** opens beside him and his students are gone. Talk to him,
  then step into the hole.
- You land on the **crater wall**. **Research Station No. 1** is on its middle ledge (Penny hides there), so is an
  **icy cave**, and the way down into the crater is on the bottom ledge.

## The crater, in two times

The crater exists twice: **in the past** and **in the future**, with their own colours, music and wild Pokémon. A
**time rift** hangs in the air in each (cold blue in the past, warm amber in the future): press <Key>A</Key>,
answer "Step through?", and you stand on the same spot in the other time. The places are the same in both:

- **Research Station No. 2**: the house on the shelf below the stairs into the crater.
- **Research Station No. 3**: the house on the knoll in the east of the crater.
- **Research Station No. 4**: above the waterfall. Surf across the pond and climb it (Waterfall).
- **Arven's picnic**: by the big lake in the south.
- **The Titans' den**: on the crater's west side.
- **The Crystal Cavern**: the cave in the crater's north-west wall.
- **The Zero Lab**: the shutter in the crater's north-east wall.

## The story

The Journal lists these steps in this order; the middle three can be done in any order.

### 1. Penny

Find **Penny** in Research Station No. 1 on the wall's middle ledge. She says where the other two went (Arven down
into the crater, Nemona off to battle anything that moves) and that the professors left logs in the stations.

### 2. Arven's sandwiches

**Arven** is at his picnic by the big lake, nursing a worn-out <Mon name="Koraidon" /> in the past and a
<Mon name="Miraidon" /> in the future. His sandwiches need **Sour Herba Mystica** from the Titans:

- In the past, beat the Titan <Mon name="Great Tusk" /> **twice** in the den on the crater's west side (Lv 68, then
  Lv 72 holding Leftovers), and bring Arven its herb: Koraidon gets up.
- Step through a time rift to the future and do the same with <Mon name="Iron Treads" /> (Lv 68, then Lv 72):
  Miraidon gets up.

A Titan battle takes no Poké Balls, and losing it does not black you out: you can try again.

### 3. Nemona

**Nemona** is in the future, in the tall grass south of the waterfall's pond, battling every Iron Paradox she
finds. Beat her.

<Team id={952} />

### 4. The four logs

Each research station holds a recorded log from the professors: play the recorder on the left counter of all four
(No. 4 is the one above the waterfall). Then talk to Penny in Station No. 1: she unlocks the **Zero Lab**'s door.

### 5. The Zero Lab

Once the door is unlocked, both legends are on their feet and Nemona is beaten, Arven and Nemona go and open the
shutter in the crater's north-east wall by themselves. Go in and talk to the machine. The professors' AIs battle
you one after the other, then the **Paradise Protection Protocol** sends out the lab's own Koraidon and Miraidon in
a Double Battle.

### 6. Home

The machine stops and everyone goes home through the hole. In Mossdeep City Jacq thanks you with a
<Item name="Master Ball" />; then he and his students go back in and live in the research stations.

## After the story

- Koraidon and Miraidon wait at the picnic spot, each in its own time, and the rest of the Gen 9 legends appear:
  three in each crater, two in the Crystal Cavern, one in the icy cave, three in the **Sealed Vault** behind the
  Crystal Cavern's second cave mouth (it opens now) and <Mon name="Terapagos" /> in the **Underdepths**, down the
  cavern's ladder. Penny, back in Station No. 1, says where.
- **Each is met once.** Caught or knocked out, it is gone for good, so save first.
- **The Sealed Vault** has a floor of polished crystal: you slide over it until something stops you, as on ice.
  Wo-Chien, Ting-Lu and Chi-Yu wait behind crystals along the back wall; out on the floor stand three stakes, marked
  with a bundle of tablets, a vessel on three legs and a string of curved beads. Come to a stop beside a stake and
  pull it, and its legend's crystal bursts. A pulled stake no longer stops you.
- **Nemona** lives in Research Station No. 3 and battles you again, with the same team.

## Items

- <Item name="Booster Energy" />: an item ball in the crater in the past, one in the crater in the future and one
  in the Crystal Cavern. The Zero Lab's supply terminal sells more, ¥5,000 each.
- <Item name="Never-Melt Ice" />: an item ball in the icy cave.
- <Item name="Master Ball" />: Jacq's thanks at the end of the story.

## The rest of Gen 9

v1.9 has every Gen 9 Pokémon, 127 slots in all, at their official Pokédex numbers (Sprigatito is No. 906, Pecharunt
No. 1025). Outside Area Zero:

- The other Gen 9 Pokémon, Paldean Wooper and the three Paldean Tauros live in the wild all over Hoenn, without
  taking any of the game's own Pokémon away. Each one's [Pokédex](/wiki/pokedex/) page says where; the
  [Pokémon finder](/wiki/pokemon/) searches them all.
- The **Lilycove Department Store, 4F**, sells the six new evolution items alongside the old ones:
  <Item name="Auspicious Armor" /> and <Item name="Malicious Armor" /> (Charcadet; ¥3,000 each),
  <Item name="Syrupy Apple" /> (Applin; ¥2,200), <Item name="Metal Alloy" /> (Duraludon; ¥3,000),
  <Item name="Unremarkable Teacup" /> (Poltchageist; ¥1,600) and <Item name="Leader's Crest" /> (Bisharp; ¥3,000).
- Older families gained their Gen 9 evolutions: Primeape → Annihilape, Girafarig → Farigiraf, Dunsparce →
  Dudunsparce, Bisharp → Kingambit, Applin → Dipplin → Hydrapple, Duraludon → Archaludon, Paldean Wooper →
  Clodsire. How each evolves here is on its Pokédex page.
- The legends' seven signature moves (Collision Course, Electro Drift, Ruination, Ivy Cudgel, Hydro Steam,
  Psyblade, Glaive Rush) have code of their own, and since v2.0 every Gen 9 ability works as it does in Scarlet and
  Violet (not Zero to Hero: there is no Hero Form) and the Gen 9 moves play animations of their own. The other new
  moves are copies of moves the game already has that work the same way; some do less than in Scarlet and Violet:
  Rage Fist and Last Respects are plain hits. Each move's and ability's description on the [Moves](/wiki/moves/) and
  [Abilities](/wiki/abilities/) pages says what it does in this game.
- **Okidogi, Munkidori, Fezandipiti, Ogerpon and Pecharunt** live in Kitakami, in the game since v2.0: once Area
  Zero's story is over, Briar waits beside the black hole in Mossdeep City with a bus there. See the
  [Kitakami guide](/guides/kitakami/).
